Order on DoorDash

Hungry now? We’ve partnered with DoorDash so you can enjoy your Fiesta Pizza favorites delivered hot and fresh or ready for pickup.

Go to DoorDash Store

Pickup & Delivery Details

  • Pickup: When ordering for pickup, head to our counter and provide your name and order number. We’ll have your pie ready and waiting.
  • Delivery zones: DoorDash delivers within most of Salem city limits. Enter your address on DoorDash to confirm.
  • Fees & ETAs: Delivery fees and estimated arrival times are set by DoorDash and may vary based on distance and demand.

Having trouble?
