Our Story

Fiesta Pizza was born in the heart of Salem with a simple mission: marry our love of pizza with the vibrant flavors of our Mexican heritage. As a family‑owned pizzeria, we’ve spent years perfecting our masa‑inspired crusts, fresh salsas and quick service so you never have to choose between quality and speed.

From the first pie we served, our community has been at the center of everything we do. We’re proud to call Salem home and work hard to make Fiesta Pizza a warm, welcoming spot for families, friends and foodies alike.
